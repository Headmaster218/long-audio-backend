# ERes2NetV2 matched two-meeting prefix screen

20 October 2026 UTC. Bounded research result; product code, model defaults and the production 512-dimensional contract are unchanged. The exact terminal result was independently audited and accepted.

## Result and denominator

The frozen screen completed **40 encodes** on the same 20 predeclared two-second observations: 20 with the current model, then 20 with ERes2NetV2. All 19 available historical current-model normalized vectors were reproduced byte-for-byte before the authentic previously missing observation was encoded as current call 20. Both original model workers and the original owner terminated successfully.

Each meeting retains ten distinct physical observations and all ten vectors in each fresh arm. Nine observations per meeting are reference-scorable; the remaining observation is overlap in rank 1 and missing reference coverage in rank 2. Both remain in the comparison population. Each meeting has 45 natural/source-independent/vector-complete pairs and 36 reference-scorable pairs. These are descriptive/research denominators; historical product-authority eligibility remains unavailable.

Pure coverage counts observations in nonsingleton, fully reference-scorable, same-speaker groups. Fragmentation is the sum, over represented reference speakers, of occupied clusters minus one, including singleton clusters. It is not a duration-weighted diarization score. Three reference speakers are represented in each final meeting population; cross-meeting physical identity is not established.

Across all **44 arm-prefix states** (two arms, two meetings, k=0 through 10), the saved metrics show zero false links, unscorable links, mixed clusters or constraint violations. V2 preserves every correct fresh-current link at the corresponding prefix and both qualified historical rank-1 final links. Rank 2 satisfies the predeclared joint coverage increase and fragmentation reduction; rank 1 has no final regression. No thresholds or grouping rules were changed after seeing candidate outputs.

This is a small, dependent two-meeting research result. Four final correct V2 links and zero observed errors do not establish a general false-merge rate. The three reserved confirmation meetings have not been used by this experiment.

## Fixed measurement and chronological protocol

The input was the automatically chosen D2 population from the predeclared 360-second channel-1 prefixes. Every crop remained exactly 32,000 mono samples at 16 kHz; both arms consumed 80 seconds of waveform in total. PCM/float hashes, source ranges, witnesses, aliases and dependencies stayed fixed.

Native inference reused the pinned Sherpa CPU backend and frontend, two configured threads, float32 input and normalization. Current vectors were 512-D and V2 vectors 192-D in separate research spaces. The pure comparator differed only in its injected dimension; the existing arithmetic, 0.55 similarity threshold, 0.10 rival margin, reciprocal/complete-link rules, cannot-links, independence requirements and all rivals were retained. No pooled-vector learning, cross-model vector comparison or product schema/dimension bypass occurred.

Fresh prefix builds used only observations admitted through each k and the corresponding immutable witness deltas. Future observations were excluded. Both complete arm predictions and their source/model/runtime bindings were sealed before interpreting the saved per-crop reference classifications. Raw reference files were not reopened. Earlier baseline label exposure was disclosed; labels did not select crops, tune parameters or enter the algorithm.

Fresh rebuilds can change previous partitions: V2 rank 1 has three correct links at k=6 and two at k=7. Corresponding-prefix safety/preservation does not prove persistent identity stability or absence of retractions.

## Preserved failures and gate scope

Three earlier owner invocations remain failed evidence: a mistyped frozen-design SHA literal; a witness-record schema comparison mismatch; and an unsupported optional `/proc/.../children` file in the executor namespace. The first two started no model worker. The third started one research worker but failed before model construction. All three produced zero encodes and retain positive original terminal evidence.

The typed-witness correction preserved existing full-row hashes. A saved-only gate checked all 20 PCM/float bridges, 34 witnesses and historical replay. The childless worker now accepts only `ECHILD` from `waitpid(-1, WNOHANG)`; unexpected children, malformed results or other errors refuse. This covers ordinary waitable direct children, not a general family census. Five injected branches and one real current-worker preconstructor gate passed through imports/config/input checks with zero encodes. The earlier V2 constructor qualification remains separate. Preparatory and failed invocations are not acoustic successes.
