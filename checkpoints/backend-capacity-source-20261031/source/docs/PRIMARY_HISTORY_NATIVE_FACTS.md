# Typed native evidence in the integrated history candidate

This is an unqualified development correction. It adds no table, model, threshold, historical backfill or global observation-count cap. The nine consolidated tables remain scopes, attempts, observations (the existing certificates table name), objects, references, dependencies, sources, decisions and decision rows.

## Atomic admission and terminal transitions

The registered live owner authenticates the exact acknowledged primary commit or acknowledged durable MOSS result with verified original cleanup and current source binding. A caller-supplied success flag is not authority. Initial claim retains basis, immutable proof/receipt objects, edges, source/dependency facts and every admitted candidate in one transaction. Immutable observation ID, header, references and preallocated invocation ID are distinct from a later vector certificate ID.

Pending rows have no vector, vector digest or certificate ID. Their sole terminal transition is to candidate_missing with those fields still NULL, or vector with exactly 2,048 bytes and a certificate bound to observation/invocation/vector digest. Only the registered fresh probe's positive cleanup permits vector completion. Pending rows cannot enter score prefixes, positive projections or retirement. Lost acknowledgement resolves storage ownership without replay or vector promotion.

Attempt state is reserved, success or failure. Every acknowledged claim retains a basis. Exact original bounded failure bytes are the failure terminal itself, rather than being wrapped into a larger envelope. Success has its separately validated shape. Terminal kind, bytes and vector identity are immutable; conflicting retries fail. An exact session/recording/token reader returns audit facts and bytes after length preflight, without repairing, revalidating files or starting work.

## Counts, charges and uncertainty

Observation counts increment once at admission. Vector counts increment only on pending-to-vector. Witness counts increment once per basis, including a zero-candidate or failed batch. Admission and terminal closure each advance history revision and clear current publication. Basis-only evidence has zero observations and zero vectors; it can contribute hard constraints but cannot invent an unknown-score rival.

A pending/missing row costs header length plus 1,024 bytes; a vector row costs header length plus 3,072. Each attempt and retained basis own 1,024 bytes each. Objects, references, dependencies and source rows retain their separate charges. Admission immediately converts all retained charges from reservation and leaves only 2,048 bytes per pending row reserved for possible vector growth. Finalization releases reservation/staging once. Source and retirement counts distinguish all observations from vector certificates and also census retained bases, including zero-node outcomes.

After all authenticated source/PCM aliases and hard constraints are processed, any independent candidate group without a measured vector produces persisted unknown_rival. It cannot disappear from ranking or improve a margin. A complete exact alias to a measured group can resolve the missing score only after conflicts and witnesses are checked. An unresolved genuine candidate may keep the recording abstaining indefinitely. No replay or omission silently repairs it; an unchanged missing-score revision is excluded from repeated work until evidence changes.

## Fixed resource boundaries

The shared staging allowance remains 8 MiB. Both new-history and legacy admission guards include one fixed 512 KiB controller-metadata reservation, rather than allocating it per scope. The bounded unknown-authority notification map uses that reservation and per-source tokens; overflow closes positive reads without growing the map. Probe ownership remains within its separate existing 512 KiB envelope.

The new MOSS route forecasts simultaneous owned batch/crop/receipt graphs, three graph/wire copies, source/dependency tuple allowances and result/control buffers before additional claim ownership. Over-envelope opportunities are refused whole before durable admission, without trimming any node or witness. Such refusal is an unadmitted opportunity, not a retained missing-score rival.

Immutable headers remain at most 2,048 bytes. The typed comparator transport envelope is separately bounded at 3,072 bytes. Public pages retain the 32-row/128 KiB ceiling including framing. Observation SQL first reads bounded scalar lengths and admits a byte-bounded prefix before copying full headers/vectors. Large proof objects retain their separate existing bound. Comparator task memory remains 128 MiB and all existing SQL/checkpoint/deadline limits remain in force.
