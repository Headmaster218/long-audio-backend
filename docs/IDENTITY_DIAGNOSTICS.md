# Offline identity-memory diagnostics

This research branch contains a pure, model-free experimental state machine plus invented low-dimensional synthetic tests. It is not imported by the service, Store, worker or enrollment endpoints. The integrated service's ordinary automatic matching does not update references.

The module explores verified anchors, retraction, negative evidence, bounded automatic observations and source deduplication. Its automatic-support strategy has a demonstrated synthetic error-amplification failure: a wrong accepted observation can widen the later acceptance region. A passing regression that reproduces this failure is evidence of the risk, not evidence of safety.

No real-person vectors, recordings, individual outcomes, benchmark caches, transcripts or clinical/voice-condition records are included. Private dataset replay scripts and results are intentionally outside this branch. The public synthetic tests do not reproduce those studies or establish population-level false-match rates.

Do not promote automatic support into production. Any future integration needs explicit source/stream/model binding, exact reviewed acoustic support, durable correction/retraction lineage, group access control, independent frozen evaluation and a separate decision. Naming or correcting a transcript segment does not certify every sample used by its voice embedding.
