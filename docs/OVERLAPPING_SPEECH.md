# Overlapping speech

The original mixture, detected simultaneous speaker activity, and optional overlap transcription candidates are distinct records. Ordinary mixture ASR may capture only the louder voice. A source slot or conditioning hint is not independently verified global identity.

Overlap backends are opt-in. MossFormer separation, English-oriented SE-DiCoW, MOSS-TD transformers and MOSS-TD CPP have different capacity, language and resource limitations. Original mixture audio remains authoritative. Unverified candidates do not enroll speakers or update identity templates.

See [MOSS-TD](MOSS_TD_SETUP.md), [CPP/GGUF](MOSS_TD_CPP_SETUP.md), [SE-DiCoW](DICOW_SETUP.md), and [API candidate ledgers](API.md). Historical measurements in setup guides have not been rerun for this public import and are not current long-duration acceptance.
