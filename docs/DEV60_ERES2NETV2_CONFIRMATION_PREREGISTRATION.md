## Outcome definitions and interpretability floor

Compute metrics per meeting and event. P is the full physical-alias-class population, with raw-record counts retained separately. A class is reference-scorable only when every constituent original crop is fully scorable and their labels agree. Never choose a favorable alias representative. S is the scorable subset; S_g contains classes with reference label g. K is the complete predicted partition including singletons. L is all predicted identity pairs within nontrivial clusters.

- Correct links T have endpoints in S with the same reference label; false links F have endpoints in S with different labels; unscorable links U have at least one endpoint outside S. These partition L. Report absolute counts, with precision |T|/(|T|+|F|), null for a zero denominator, and U separately.
- Mixed-reference nontrivial clusters contain at least two distinct known labels. Unscorable-membership clusters contain any class outside S. Report both counts; a mixed merge is a safety failure even if it reduces fragmentation.
- A pure nontrivial cluster has at least two distinct physical classes, all scorable and agreeing. Let C_pure count the classes in these clusters. Primary coverage is Q=C_pure/|S|, null if |S|=0. Also report C_pure/|P|, |S|/|P| and raw original-observation denominators. Both arms share the same fixed reference denominator at each matched event.
- For each represented reference speaker g, k_g counts all predicted clusters intersecting S_g, including singleton and mixed/unscorable-membership intersections. Excess fragmentation is E=sum_g(k_g−1), null with no scorable speaker. Report each k_g and represented-speaker count; do not hide singleton fragmentation.
- Constraint/uncertainty violations include crossing an authenticated cannot-link or prohibited dependency, inconsistent alias/proof binding, missing-rival bypass, dropped competitor, or any merge whose full trace fails unchanged score/floor/margin/reciprocity/three-way-independence rules. Self-conflicting native witnesses are explicit invariant failures.

Unavailable or invalid decisions, unknown rivals, incomplete constraints/provenance, or unbound classifications produce null relevant link/coverage/fragmentation outcomes, not zero errors, empty-link success or synthetic singletons. Independently known input/vector/reference counts can still be reported. A valid ready empty-link result with bound references has known T=F=U=0 and C_pure=0; precision and reference-empty denominators remain null. Do not infer safety from unavailable outcomes.

Each meeting must have at least three distinct, dependency-screened mutually independent physical observations with vectors in both arms. It must also have at least one reference-scorable same-speaker pair opportunity and one reference-scorable different-speaker pair opportunity among vector-complete, source-independent observations. Physical alias copies do not inflate the floor. Independence here is evidence/dependency screening, not statistical independence. Reference-defined adequacy is evaluated only after the global barrier. This is an interpretation floor, not a comparator threshold, cluster minimum, admission change or permission to select additional data. Insufficiency in any meeting is INCONCLUSIVE.

## Frozen advancement and failure rules

Advancement requires all of the following:

1. Every source, chronology, exact-control, completeness, resource and positive terminal gate passes; every required prefix has a qualified decision status. Every meeting meets the information floor.
2. At every prefix/final event, V2 has exactly zero false links, unscorable links, mixed-reference clusters and constraint/uncertainty-handling violations. Zero is absolute, not relative to current.
3. Every correct fresh-current link is also correct in V2 at the corresponding prefix. Preserve qualified historical product final correct links separately where known; unavailable historical links are unknown, not an empty preservation set. Compare pair membership, not cluster identifier strings.
4. Final Q and E are nonnull in both arms for all three meetings. At least one meeting has strictly greater V2 coverage AND strictly lower fragmentation; neither measure worsens in either other meeting. Use exact integer/rational comparisons. No aggregate gain may hide a per-meeting regression.

Any observed safety violation or failure of the benefit rule is NO-GO. Missing required evidence, operational failure or insufficient information is BLOCKED/INCONCLUSIVE, also not advancement. No meeting may be dropped. Preserve the exact reason and all planned denominators. There is no outcome-driven tuning, reselection, retry or deadline renewal. After references are interpreted, this confirmation set is spent for outcome-designed revision.

Report cross-prefix link additions/retractions, cluster splits and coverage changes descriptively. The earlier screen lost a correct V2 edge between rank-1 prefixes 6 and 7 despite matched-prefix preservation. The frozen success rule therefore establishes neither monotone retention nor stable identities. Do not add a sticky-identity policy or silently introduce an outcome-dependent stability gate.
